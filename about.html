About me

I am a PhD Scholar in the Department of Metallurgical and Materials Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Madras, India. My research interests center on semiconductor nanofabrication, thin-film materials, surface engineering and nanoscale characterization.

I am interested in understanding how processing conditions, material selection and interfaces influence the structural, surface and functional properties of thin-film systems.

Academic background

  • PhD — Metallurgical and Materials Engineering, IIT Madras (ongoing)
  • M.S. — Metallurgical and Materials Engineering, IIT Madras, 2024
  • B.E. — Mechanical Engineering, Thiagarajar College of Engineering, 2022

Research approach

I combine experimental materials processing with advanced characterization and computational insights to develop and evaluate materials and fabrication approaches.
