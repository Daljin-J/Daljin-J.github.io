Surface engineering · Published work

Zinc-assisted sub-nanometer surface smoothing

Exploring a material-addition strategy in which deposited zinc fills nanoscale surface valleys, reducing roughness on silicon and glass substrates rather than relying only on conventional material removal.

Selected result: On silicon, Rq decreased from 0.717 nm to 0.129 nm; on glass, Rq decreased from 0.590 nm to 0.174 nm in the reported experiments.
