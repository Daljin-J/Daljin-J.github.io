Thin-film fabrication

Method for obtaining free-standing ultrathin films using a sacrificial layer

A process concept for manufacturing a free-standing multilayer thin film by selectively removing a sacrificial layer. The platform is intended to enable release of thin-film architectures while retaining the functional film stack.
